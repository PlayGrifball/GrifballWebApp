- **Generated At:** 2026-10-31T05:28:04.012Z
- **Total Links Processed:** 65

- **Total Worksheet Tabs Extracted:** 792
- **Total Non-Empty Data Rows Extracted:** 85,611

## Directory Structure

- `raw/` — Original `.xlsx` workbooks, `.htmlview.html` snapshots, `.wayback.html` Internet Archive captures, and `.csv` exports (52+ MB).
- `csv/<Category>/<source-id>/<sheetIndex>_<sheetName>.csv` — Every single worksheet tab from all 51 workbooks exported as clean UTF-8 CSV (792 CSV files).
- `json/<Category>/<source-id>.json` — Full multi-tab structured JSON representation of each workbook.
- `compiled/` — High-level normalized datasets:
  - `catalog.json` — Master index of all 65 sources, 792 sheets, row/column counts, visibility state, and sample headers.
  - `agla-champions.json` — All 76 championship teams/rosters (2008–2026) and 264 ring-holders leaderboard.
  - `all-time-infinite-stats.json` — All-time Halo Infinite career stats for 107 players + S1–S7 seasonal & playoff breakdowns.
  - `late-league-master.json` — Late League master player registry (131 players with Gamertags, Discord IDs, locations, systems), Data Center Pings (51 players), Grifbux MMR (465 entries), and S6–S8 leaderboards.
  - `youtube-vods.json` — Channel metadata & video IDs extracted from the Grifball YouTube channel.
  - `waypoint-matches.json` — Halo Infinite seasons' match IDs (Waypoint, Halo Data Hive and HaloQuery links in the game history sheets), keyed by each season's `HistoricalSourceID` (`{sourceWorkbook}/{sourceSheet}`), split into regular season and playoffs. Generated by `node archive-tools/extract-waypoint-matches.mjs`; `archive-tools/import-archive.mjs` uploads it so the import derives those seasons' stats from the matches.

## Recovery Notes on Dead / Restricted Links

1. **2018 SL18 Pro/Legacy/Ammy Schedules (`DEAD`) & `grifballstats.com/AGLASML18Stats.php`:**
   - While the 3 Google Sheets return HTTP `410 Gone` and `grifballstats.com` is offline, **`2018-sml18-fantasy-rey` (`SML18 Fantasy by Rey`)** contains **19 tabs**, including `03_Fixtures_Results.csv` (`AGLA SL18 Fixtures & Results`, 75 rows), `06_Total_Stats.csv` (33 rows), and weekly `W1`–`W4` Stats / Points / Team Points tabs.
   - **Caveat:** the `W3` and `W4` tabs are byte-identical copies of `W2` (and `W1 Points` is identical to `Points System`), so only `W1`–`W2` appear to contain distinct weekly data. There are no `W5`–`W8` tabs.
2. **2018 FL18 Schedule (`DEAD`):**
   - Permanently deleted (`410 Gone`), but all team rosters, captains, and bids are preserved in **`2018-fl18-bid-league` (`FL18 Bid League`)**, and the champion (`One Hit Wonders`) is recorded in `agla-champions.json`.
3. **2019 WL19 Playoff Bracket (`challonge.com/h3wl19`):**
   - Challonge blocks automated requests via Cloudflare (`403`), but **`2019-wl19-schedule-stats` (`WL19 Schedule & Stats`)** preserves the playoff stats (`03_Playoff_Stats.csv`, 64 rows), regular season schedule (`01_Schedule.csv`, 58 rows), and per-game results (`14_Game_Results.csv`, 3,415 rows). The bracket itself is not recovered.
4. **2021 Fall League (`FL21`) Bonus Discovery:**
   - Although not listed as a separate link, **`2021-sml21-pro-stats`** and **`2021-sml21-ammy-stats`** contain `FL21_Stats`, `FL21_PlayoffStats`, and `FL21_MatchHistory` tabs (353 and 586 match rows).
5. **2022 ASpence Halo 4 Bid Season Draft (`DEAD`):**
   - The draft sheet is `410 Gone`, but **`2022-h4-bid-sheet`** (28 tabs) and **`2022-h4-bid-stats`** (19 tabs) preserve all 4 bidding rounds (`Round 1`–`Round 4`), `Bid Values`, `Signed`, `AGLA Pro/Ammy Season Raw Data`, team rosters (`Teams`), and match history (`Match_History`, 814 rows).
6. **2024 IGL Infektions Halo Infinite FL24 Stats (`1drv.ms`):**
   - OneDrive personal link requires Microsoft authentication, but **`2024-igl-evolved-fl24-stats`** (2.17 MB `.xlsx`) preserves the IGL Fall 2024 stats: `IGL Fall Season 2024 Stats Shee` (50 rows), `IGL Fall Season 2024 Sheet hist` (820 rows), `PlayerGrades+`, `TeamInfo`, and `Review HI_MatchH` (854 rows).
   - **Note:** the same-named `IGL Fall Season 2024 Stats Shee` tabs in the 2025 Octane/Classic stats workbooks contain only a header row.
7. **PlayGrifball.com Website (`http://playgrifball.com/`):**
   - Live domain is parked/404, so our downloader queried the **Internet Archive Wayback Machine CDX API** and saved an archived snapshot (`raw/website-playgrifball.wayback.html`).
8. **Late League Website (`lateleague.org`):**
   - `raw/late-league-website.html` is only the empty single-page-app shell (no rendered content); no data was recovered from it.
